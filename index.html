About me

My story

I started teaching before I started coding. In 2021 I began volunteering with a church youth program, teaching math, English and study skills to students from grade 2 to grade 12. In 2023 I turned that into my own tutoring business, and today I work with 7 students, building their worksheets and lesson plans myself.

Teaching taught me something I didn't expect: the hardest part of any problem is making it simple for the person in front of you. That's the same thing good software does. When I got to Mount Royal and started building web apps and databases, I realized I was solving the same kind of problem, just with code.

Now I'm in my third year of Computer Science with a Data Science minor, in the co-op program, and I'm working toward a career building AI. My plan is to get real industry experience through co-op, then apply to the AI master's program at Queen's University, and one day start my own tech company.

    Volunteer Teacher, Church Youth Program

    Taught math, English and study skills to students in grades 2 to 12. This is where I first learned that teaching is mostly listening: figuring out exactly where someone is stuck before trying to explain anything.

  2. 2023 – Present
